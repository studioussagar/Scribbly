About Scribbly

A community-driven blogging platform where readers become writers, and every story finds its audience.

What is Scribbly?

Scribbly is a modern blogging platform built for storytellers, thinkers, and curious minds. Whether you're here to read insightful posts, engage with ideas through comments, or share your own perspective with the world — Scribbly is built for you.

Content is organised by categories, and every post supports rich formatting thanks to an integrated rich-text editor (TinyMCE), giving authors full creative control over how their stories look.

  • Rich-text blog creation with TinyMCE
  • Post categories for easy discovery
  • Like & Dislike system on every post
  • Threaded comment replies
  • Real-time chat between users
  • Dark / Light theme toggle
  • Author request system for viewers
  • Responsive — works on any device

How It Works

Sign Up

Create a free account as a Viewer to start reading and engaging with posts.

Explore

Browse posts by category, like content you enjoy, comment your thoughts, and share posts.

Try Writing

Viewers can submit a trial blog for review. If it resonates, you can request Author access.

Publish

Authors can publish rich blogs instantly, manage their posts from the dashboard, and grow their audience.

User Roles

Viewer

The default role for every new user.

  • Read all published blog posts
  • Like, dislike and comment on posts
  • Share posts with others
  • Chat with other users in real time
  • Submit a trial blog for review
  • Request to become an Author

Author

Earned by submitting a post that resonates with the community.

  • Everything a Viewer can do
  • Create and publish rich-text blog posts
  • Upload images in blog content
  • Manage posts from the Author Dashboard
  • View likes, dislikes and recent comments at a glance
  • Edit or delete their own posts
