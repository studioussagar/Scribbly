# Scribbly Community & Content Guidelines

**Status:** Draft for Review  
**Applies to:** All Scribbly members, writers, comments, profiles, and community interactions.

## 1. What Scribbly Is

Scribbly is a community writing platform where people can share original writing, experiences, ideas, knowledge, and perspectives. The platform is intended to encourage thoughtful publishing and constructive interaction rather than engagement driven only by popularity, promotion, or controversy.

## 2. What We Encourage

Members are encouraged to:

- Publish original and meaningful writing.
- Share personal experiences, tutorials, opinions, reviews, and stories.
- Give constructive feedback and respect differences in opinion.
- Discuss difficult subjects without attacking other people.
- Distinguish personal experience from factual claims where appropriate.
- Credit sources and other creators when their work is referenced.
- Report content that appears to violate Scribbly policies.
- Help maintain a welcoming environment for new writers.

## 3. How Members Should Interact

Members should communicate respectfully. Disagreement is expected; personal attacks are not. Avoid:

- Harassment or targeted abuse.
- Threats or encouragement of violence.
- Repeated unwanted contact.
- Hate directed at protected groups.
- Deliberate humiliation of another member.
- Publishing another person’s private information.
- Coordinated attempts to manipulate or disrupt the community.
- Abusive or intentionally hostile behavior.

## 4. Originality and Attribution

Submit work you have the right to publish. Provide attribution when using ideas, quotations, images, data, or other material from other sources. Plagiarism, impersonation, and deliberate misrepresentation of another person’s work may result in moderation action.

## 5. Allowed Content (Overview)

Scribbly generally allows:

- Personal experiences and creative writing.
- Tutorials, educational and technical content.
- Reviews and recommendations based on genuine experience.
- Opinions and commentary.
- Political and social discussion that is non-violent and non-hateful.
- Satire, fiction, and parody.
- AI-assisted or AI-generated writing, as long as it follows all policies.

## 6. Spam and Low-Value Content

Spam includes content or behavior that repeatedly distributes substantially identical, irrelevant, or low-value material, such as:

- Publishing the same or substantially similar article multiple times.
- Keyword-stuffed posts meant to manipulate discovery.
- Mass-produced low-value posts with little meaningful content.
- Repeated promotional posts or repetitive comments intended to manipulate engagement.

Accidental duplicates or minor repetition by themselves do not automatically constitute spam.

## 7. Promotion and Commercial Content

Genuine editorial content that involves products, businesses, services, destinations, or organizations is allowed, such as:

- A travel experience.
- A restaurant review based on a genuine visit.
- A product review based on actual use.
- Educational discussion of a product or company.

Content may be restricted when its primary purpose is deceptive or excessive promotion, including:

- Repeated promotional articles.
- Advertising disguised as ordinary editorial content.
- Fake or paid-for reviews presented as neutral.
- Misleading claims intended to drive purchases.
- Repeated calls to purchase, subscribe, or contact a business without meaningful editorial value.

Affiliate or referral links must not be used deceptively and may be limited when they create spam or safety issues.

## 8. Harmful, Violent, and Illegal Content

Content that encourages, celebrates, or meaningfully facilitates violence against people is prohibited or subject to escalation.

Not allowed:

- Threats or calls for violence.
- Promotion of terrorism or violent extremism.
- Detailed instructions that meaningfully facilitate real-world harm.
- Content intended to facilitate scams, fraud, impersonation, phishing, or other harmful deception.

Discussion of violence or crime in legitimate contexts (e.g., history, journalism, fiction, education) may be allowed when it does not function as encouragement or operational instructions.

## 9. Harassment and Hate

Content targeting an individual or protected group with serious abuse, threats, or hateful attacks may be removed or escalated.

Allowed: criticism of ideas, organizations, governments, public figures, or political positions, as long as it does not cross into targeted harassment or hate. Context matters.

## 10. Political and Social Content

Political and social discussion is allowed when it is presented as genuine discussion, commentary, analysis, reporting, or personal opinion. Scribbly does not prohibit a piece simply because it expresses a political opinion.

Political content must not:

- Encourage violence.
- Threaten individuals or groups.
- Promote terrorism or violent extremism.
- Target people with hateful abuse.
- Deliberately impersonate or deceive readers.
- Become coordinated spam or propaganda activity intended to manipulate the platform.

## 11. Satire, Fiction, and Humor

Satire, parody, fiction, and humor are allowed. Moderation should consider context before treating fictional statements as factual claims.

Labeling something as satire does not protect otherwise prohibited conduct such as threats, targeted harassment, hate, or serious fraud.

## 12. Profanity and Mature Language

Profanity by itself is not automatically a violation. Occasional profanity used naturally in a story, review, or conversation may be acceptable.

Profanity becomes a concern when:

- It targets a person or group as harassment or hateful abuse.
- It is combined with threats.
- It is used excessively in a way that substantially harms the community environment.

## 13. AI-Assisted and AI-Generated Writing

AI-assisted and AI-generated writing is allowed. Using AI does not automatically make content prohibited or unsafe.

However:

- AI detection is treated as a risk signal, not proof of wrongdoing.
- Writers remain responsible for the content they publish.
- AI-generated content must still follow all Scribbly policies.
- AI must not be used to generate scams, harassment, malicious instructions, or other prohibited material.

## 14. Enforcement and Appeals

Scribbly may take action when content or behavior violates these guidelines. Possible actions include:

- Informational warning.
- Content review.
- Content removal or restriction.
- Temporary loss of publishing privileges.
- Loss of trusted-writer status.
- Account suspension or other appropriate restrictions.

AI systems may assist moderation, but AI-generated risk scores or flags are not, by themselves, final proof of a violation. Where appropriate, administrators may review flagged content and consider context before taking action. Members may contact Scribbly when they believe a moderation action was incorrect.

## 15. Reporting

Members should report content that appears to violate Scribbly policies rather than attempting to punish the author themselves. False or malicious reporting may itself be treated as abuse.

## 16. Changes to These Guidelines

These guidelines may evolve as Scribbly develops. Material changes should be communicated to members when they significantly affect how content or accounts are moderated.
