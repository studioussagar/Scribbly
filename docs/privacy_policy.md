# Scribbly Privacy Policy

**Status:** Draft for Review  
**Important:** This document is a project draft and is not legal advice. A public deployment should have this policy reviewed for the jurisdictions in which Scribbly operates.

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Scribbly may collect, why it is used, and how it may be processed. The final policy should be aligned with the actual production infrastructure.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Scribbly may process:

### Account Information

- Username.
- Email address where required.
- Authentication information.
- Profile information.

### Content

- Blog titles and content.
- Comments and community interactions.
- Writer applications.
- Content submitted for AI analysis.

### Platform Activity and Logs

- Publishing activity and application status.
- Moderation events and writer status.
- Technical logs required to operate and secure the service.

### Moderation and Risk Data

- Risk scores, risk levels, and risk flags.
- Analysis summaries.
- Decision recommendations.
- Review actions and timestamps.

## 3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

- Provide Scribbly functionality and publish content.
- Authenticate users and manage accounts.
- Process writer applications and status.
- Perform AI-assisted analysis and spam/harm detection.
- Maintain platform security and reliability.
- Communicate important account or moderation events.
- Improve Scribbly’s features over time.

## 4. AI Processing

Writer applications and other submitted content may be processed by AI systems, including external AI providers when an AI-powered feature is used. AI systems receive only the text and contextual information necessary to perform the requested analysis.

Scribbly should configure providers and infrastructure in accordance with its privacy and security requirements.

## 5. Data Sharing

Scribbly should not share personal information unnecessarily. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the platform, such as infrastructure, databases, email services, authentication, analytics, or AI providers.

## 6. Public Content

Content you intentionally publish on Scribbly may be publicly visible. You should avoid publishing personal information you do not want to be public.

## 7. Data Retention

Data should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, legitimate operational requirements, dispute handling, security, or applicable legal obligations.

Before production deployment, the policy should define approximate retention periods (for example, how long logs, moderation records, and inactive accounts are kept).

## 8. Security

Scribbly should use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## 9. User Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law and your location, you may have rights relating to:

- Accessing certain personal information.
- Correcting inaccurate information.
- Deleting information or your account.
- Restricting or objecting to certain processing.
- Obtaining a copy of certain information.

The final implementation should provide a clear process for applicable requests, such as a contact address or in-product tools.

## 10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Scribbly may use cookies or similar technologies necessary for:

- Authentication and session management.
- Security.
- User preferences.

Any analytics or advertising technologies should be documented before production deployment.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

The production service should define its minimum age and any additional safeguards required for younger users, in line with applicable law.

## 12. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Scribbly’s functionality, infrastructure, or legal obligations change. Material changes should be communicated appropriately.

## 13. Contact

Privacy-related questions or requests should be directed through the privacy contact mechanism provided by Scribbly.
